# 4-Minute Hackathon Presentation Demo Script 🎬

## Timing & Flow

| Timestamp | Phase | Presenter Script & Action |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **0:00 - 0:30** | **The Problem** | *"Millions of micro-savers in India participate in informal community committees. Digital members pay via UPI, but cash-based members are left out of formal credit scoring. ChitTrust + CashBridge connects both on one shared ledger."* |
| **0:30 - 1:00** | **Group Creation & Onboarding** | *Action: Organizer creates ₹10,000 monthly committee (Shakti Committee) and invites digital member Sita & cash member Rahul with assigned CashBridge Agent Suresh Patel.* |
